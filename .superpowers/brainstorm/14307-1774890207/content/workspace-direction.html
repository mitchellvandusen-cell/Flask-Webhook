Workspace Simplification — Which direction?

Both keep all features. The question is how the space is organized.

A

Progressive Disclosure

Two columns by default. Middle column (Lead Intelligence) is hidden until you click a contact — then it slides in. The phone stays on the right. Dialer controls stay in the actions bar below the contact list.

── DEFAULT ──
[ Contacts 40% ] [ iPhone 60% ]
── CONTACT CLICKED ──
[ Contacts 30% ] [ Intel 35% ] [ iPhone 35% ]
Dial / Queue / Move buttons stay in the contacts actions bar. Dial mode toggle stays at top.
B

Phone-Centric (App Paradigm)

The iPhone IS the workspace. Contacts list on left. Phone on right — and the dialer controls (Queue, Dial, Move) live inside the phone as a "Dialer" app on the home screen. Dial Mode toggle sits above the phone's status bar.

── ALWAYS ──
[ Contacts 40% ] [ Phone 60% ]
── PHONE HOME SCREEN APPS ──
Dialer · KPIs · Messages · Calls
── DIAL MODE (above phone) ──
[ HUMAN ] [ AI ] ← tap to expand
Cleanest surface area. Everything has a home inside the phone metaphor. Lead Intel shows as a card inside the Dialer app when a contact is selected.
C

Hybrid — Both of the above

Two columns default (Contacts + Phone). Middle Intel panel slides in on contact click (like A). Dialer controls move into the phone (like B). Best of both: phone metaphor is the star, Intel appears when relevant.

── DEFAULT ──
[ Contacts 40% ] [ Phone 60% ]
Phone shows Dialer app + Dial Mode
── CONTACT CLICKED ──
[ Contacts 30% ] [ Intel 30% ] [ Phone 40% ]
Intel panel slides in · Phone still active
Most coherent story: the phone is the hero, Intel is contextual. Nothing is buried, nothing is always in the way.
