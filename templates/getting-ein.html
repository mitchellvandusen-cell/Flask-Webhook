How to Get Your Free EIN
from the IRS

An EIN (Employer Identification Number) is a free federal tax ID issued by the IRS. Sole proprietors need one to register phone numbers for spam protection — and it protects your SSN from ever being shared with carriers.

Apply for EIN on IRS.gov What is an EIN?

Why You Need an EIN as a Sole Proprietor

Required by the carrier trust network to verify your business identity for spam protection registration
Keeps your Social Security Number private — you never share your SSN with any carrier or registration service
Accepted everywhere a business tax ID is needed — banks, vendors, A2P 10DLC, CNAM, Voice Integrity
100% free from the IRS — no cost, no annual fee, yours instantly after the online application
IRS Online EIN Application Hours: Monday–Friday, 7:00 a.m. – 10:00 p.m. Eastern Time. The application is not available on weekends or federal holidays. Your EIN is issued immediately at the end of the online session.
Step-by-Step Instructions
1

Go to the IRS EIN Online Application

The IRS provides a free, secure online application at IRS.gov. No account creation required — you fill out a short form and receive your EIN immediately.

IRS EIN Online Application →
2

Click "Apply Online Now"

On the IRS page, click the "Apply Online Now" button to start the EIN Assistant. Read through the instructions and click "Begin Application" to proceed.

The IRS EIN Assistant will open in a new tab. Do not close the browser during the application — your session expires after 15 minutes of inactivity.
3

Select "Sole Proprietor" as Your Entity Type

The first question asks what type of entity you are. Select Sole Proprietor. On the next screen, select the reason for applying — choose "Banking Purposes" or "Started a New Business" (both are valid for carrier registration).

IRS: Sole Proprietorships Info →
4

Enter Your Personal Information

You will need to provide:

  • Your full legal name (first, middle initial, last)
  • Your Social Security Number (SSN) — used only to verify your identity with the IRS; never shared with carriers
  • Your mailing address (home or business)
  • Your phone number and state
Your SSN is only used by the IRS to issue your EIN. Once you have your EIN, you use the EIN (not your SSN) for all business registrations including spam protection.
5

Review and Submit

Review all the information on the confirmation screen. Make sure your legal name matches exactly how it appears on your Social Security card or IRS records. Click "Submit" to finalize.

6

Save Your EIN — It's Issued Instantly

Your EIN is displayed immediately on the confirmation page in the format XX-XXXXXXX (two digits, hyphen, seven digits). Write it down and download the confirmation PDF. You can use it right away — no waiting period.

Lost your EIN? Look it up here →
Download and save the IRS EIN confirmation letter (CP-575). You will not need to upload it to InsuranceGrokBot — just enter the EIN number in the registration form.
Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need an EIN if I already have a business license?
A business license and an EIN are separate. A business license is issued by your state or city. An EIN is issued by the IRS as a federal tax identification number. You need an EIN specifically for carrier trust network registration — a business license alone is not sufficient.
I already have an EIN from a previous business — can I use it?
Yes, as long as the EIN is registered to you or your current business entity. Each sole proprietor gets one EIN per SSN. If you already have one from a prior venture, use the same EIN — you do not need a new one. You can verify your existing EIN at IRS.gov.
Is there a cost to get an EIN?
No. An EIN from the IRS is completely free. Be cautious of third-party websites that charge a fee to "apply for an EIN" on your behalf — go directly to IRS.gov to get yours for free.
What if I do not have a Social Security Number?
Without an SSN, you cannot apply online. You would need to apply by mail using IRS Form SS-4 and provide an ITIN (Individual Taxpayer Identification Number) instead. Processing by mail can take 4–6 weeks.
What do I enter in InsuranceGrokBot once I have my EIN?
Go back to your dashboard → Numbers tab → Spam Protection → Business Profile. Select "Sole Proprietorship" as your business type, answer "Yes" to the EIN question, and enter your EIN in the format XX-XXXXXXX. Your legal name (as registered with the IRS) goes in the Business Name field.

Got your EIN? You're ready to register.

Head back to your dashboard and complete your spam protection registration. It only takes a minute.
