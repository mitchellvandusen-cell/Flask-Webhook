Enterprise-Grade Dialing

The Predictive Dialer That Actually Predicts.

Not just multi-line calling. Real Erlang-C queuing theory, real-time TCPA throttling, and per-contact timezone enforcement. The same algorithms used by enterprise contact centers — built for insurance agencies.

Erlang-C Pacing Algorithm

Your dial ratio isn't a guess. The system models your call center as an M/M/N queue — arrival rate, service rate, and agent availability — then uses binary search across 20 iterations to find the maximum dial ratio that keeps predicted abandonment under 3%.

  • M/M/N queue model with traffic intensity in Erlangs
  • Log-space arithmetic for numerical stability
  • Real-time inputs: connect rate, handle time, wrap-up
  • Auto-adjusts recommended lines (1-4) continuously

TCPA Auto-Throttle

The FTC's Telemarketing Sales Rule requires abandon rates below 3%. Our rolling 30-day tracker monitors every call outcome in real time. When your rate hits 80% of the limit (2.4%), the system automatically reduces the dial ratio — no manual intervention needed.

  • Rolling 30-day abandon rate tracking
  • Auto-throttle at 80% of 3% safe harbor
  • 1-hour real-time monitoring window
  • DB-bootstrapped from call history on startup
Recipient Timezone Enforcement

Every contact's area code is mapped to their timezone. Calls are blocked if outside 8am-9pm in the recipient's local time — not just yours.

Recording Consent Tracking

12 two-party consent states (CA, CT, FL, IL, etc.) auto-detected by area code. Consent requirements flagged before the call connects.

Agent State Machine

ACD-style agent tracking — Ready, Not Ready, On Call, Wrap-Up, Break. The pacing algorithm knows how many agents are available in real time.

Callback Queue

Busy, no-answer, and voicemail contacts are automatically queued for re-dial. Duplicate prevention. 24-hour auto-cleanup.
