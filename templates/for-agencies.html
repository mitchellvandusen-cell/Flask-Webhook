Enterprise-Grade Dialing

The Predictive Dialer That Actually Predicts.

Not just multi-line calling. The system learns your connect rates, automatically adjusts how many lines to dial, and keeps you compliant with TCPA regulations — no spreadsheets, no manual tracking, no guesswork.

AI-Optimized Dial Pacing

Your dial ratio isn't a guess. The system analyzes your team's real connect rates, average handle time, and agent availability to calculate exactly how many lines to dial — maximizing conversations while keeping abandonment below the 3% safe harbor.

  • Learns from your team's actual call data
  • Factors in connect rate, handle time, and wrap-up
  • Auto-adjusts recommended lines (1-4) continuously
  • More conversations per hour without over-dialing

TCPA Auto-Throttle

The FTC's Telemarketing Sales Rule requires abandon rates below 3%. The system monitors every call outcome with a rolling 30-day tracker. When your rate approaches the limit, it automatically reduces the dial ratio — no manual intervention needed.

  • Rolling 30-day abandon rate tracking
  • Auto-throttle before you hit the 3% limit
  • Real-time monitoring — not end-of-day reports
  • Stay compliant without thinking about it
Recipient Timezone Enforcement

Every contact's area code is mapped to their timezone. Calls are blocked if outside 8am-9pm in the recipient's local time — not just yours.

Recording Consent Tracking

12 two-party consent states (CA, CT, FL, IL, etc.) auto-detected by area code. Consent requirements flagged before the call connects.

Agent State Machine

Track every agent's status — Ready, Not Ready, On Call, Wrap-Up, Break. The dialer knows who's available and adjusts pacing automatically.

Callback Queue

Busy, no-answer, and voicemail contacts are automatically queued for re-dial. Duplicate prevention. 24-hour auto-cleanup.
