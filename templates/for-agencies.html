The Real Problem

You're Running an Agency in the Dark.

You hired good people. You gave them leads. But you have no idea what's happening between "here are your leads" and "I didn't close any this week." The gap between those two moments is where revenue dies.

You Don't Know Who's Dialing

Is Sarah making 100 calls a day or 15? Is Mike's connect rate 8% because his numbers are flagged, or because he's calling at 7pm? You shouldn't have to ask.

Warm Leads Are Calling Back and Nobody Answers

Your agents texted 200 people. 30 called back. 8 got voicemail because the agent was on another call. Those 8 people aren't calling again. That's revenue you'll never see.

Coaching Is a Guess

"How's it going?" isn't coaching. Without per-agent connect rates, average call duration, and conversation quality data, you're just hoping your agents figure it out on their own.
