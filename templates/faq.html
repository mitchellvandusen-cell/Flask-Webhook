Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about InsuranceGrokBot.

{# ── General ── #}

InsuranceGrokBot is an all-in-one AI sales platform built specifically for life insurance agents. It combines AI-powered SMS texting, an AI voice agent, a multi-line power dialer, predictive dialing, smart lead scoring, workflow automation, spam protection, and team management into a single dashboard. It handles lead engagement autonomously — texting, calling, qualifying, handling objections, and booking appointments — so you can focus on closing.
{# ── SMS Bot ── #}

The bot integrates with your CRM and monitors incoming leads. When a lead responds, the AI analyzes the entire conversation history to understand context, intent, and emotional tone. It references a knowledge base of 270+ insurance carriers, underwriting rules, and proven sales strategies to craft human-sounding responses. It handles objections using a two-phase framework, detects booking intent, and schedules appointments on your calendar automatically.
{# ── Voice Agent ── #}

The AI Voice Agent makes and receives phone calls using real-time voice AI. It sounds like a real person, not a robocall. It can qualify leads, handle objections, discuss insurance products intelligently, and book appointments — all by voice. You can listen to live calls and intercept (take over) at any time. When multiple calls connect simultaneously, overflow calls are handled by the AI voice agent while you talk to the first pickup.
{# ── Power Dialer ── #}

The Power Dialer is a built-in multi-line dialer that lets you call up to 4 contacts simultaneously. It includes AI-powered Smart Filters that automatically classify your leads as Hot, Warm, Cool, or Cold based on actual conversation analysis — not simple rules. Features include call recording, one-click voicemail drop, live call monitoring, contact intelligence panels, and calling hours enforcement.
{# ── Predictive Dialing ── #}

Predictive Dialing uses an Erlang-C algorithm to automatically calculate the optimal number of lines to dial based on your historical connect rate. It auto-paces throughout your session, adjusting in real time as your connect rate changes. It includes TCPA compliance tracking with a rolling 3% abandon rate cap, recipient timezone enforcement, two-party recording consent detection, and a callback queue for scheduled re-dials.
{# ── Smart Filters ── #}

Smart Filters read every contact's full conversation history and classify them by temperature (Hot, Warm, Cool, Cold), engagement score (0–100), and whether you should respond right now. The scoring reads what leads actually said — a lead who said "not interested" gets classified as Cold, not Hot just because they replied. Contacts are automatically re-scored on every dialer load and after every new message.
{# ── Objection Handling ── #}

InsuranceGrokBot uses an elite closer's mindset with 290+ objection phrases across six categories: Not Interested, Spouse/Partner, Price/Money, Already Covered, Think About It, and Busy/Timing. It uses a two-phase framework — Phase 1 solves the practical side with logistical questions, Phase 2 (triggered after 2+ same objections) addresses the underlying fear. Sales objections like "not interested" are never treated as opt-outs — only explicit TCPA stop words end the conversation.
{# ── Appointments ── #}

Absolutely. Both the SMS bot and the AI Voice Agent can book appointments. The system reads your real-time calendar availability, never double-books, and verifies the prospect's intent before confirming. It works with GoHighLevel calendars and Google Calendar. After booking, your CRM automatically sends the calendar invite.
{# ── Workflows ── #}

Workflows let you build automated sequences triggered by events like new contact created, SMS received, lead going cold, or no response after a set number of days. Each workflow can send SMS, make AI calls, add tags, update fields, check AI intelligence conditions, and more. Four pre-built templates are included: Speed to Lead, Aged Lead Re-engagement, SMS Response Handler, and Re-engage Cold Leads.
{# ── Spam Protection ── #}

Comprehensive spam protection including CNAM (Caller Name) registration so your business name displays on caller ID, Voice Integrity registration with AT&T/Hiya, T-Mobile, and Verizon to remediate spam labels, STIR/SHAKEN attestation for carrier trust, A2P 10DLC registration for SMS deliverability, and smart number rotation that automatically rests flagged numbers. All managed from one dashboard.

{# ── Human Identity ── #}

No. Both the SMS bot and the AI Voice Agent are designed to sound completely human. The system enforces a strict human identity protocol — it never reveals that it is AI, never uses words like "system," "technical issue," or "automated," and strips any AI-sounding language before sending. Leads believe they are texting or speaking with a real insurance advisor.
{# ── Compliance ── #}

InsuranceGrokBot takes compliance seriously. TCPA stop words (stop, unsubscribe, cancel, opt out, do not call) immediately halt messaging. The predictive dialer enforces a rolling 3% abandon rate cap, calling hours restrictions with timezone awareness, and two-party recording consent detection for all 12 applicable states. A2P 10DLC registration is built in for SMS deliverability compliance.
