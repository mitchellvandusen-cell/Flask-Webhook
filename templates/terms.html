2. Description of Service

InsuranceGrokBot is a white-label AI-powered sales automation and dialing platform built exclusively for life insurance agents and agencies. The Service connects to your CRM via OAuth and includes the following features depending on your subscription tier:

  • AI SMS Handler: Outbound and inbound SMS automation. Handles objections using a 290+ phrase classification engine, maintains full conversation context across 20+ message turns, and books calendar appointments autonomously. Powered by xAI Grok with 270+ insurance carrier knowledge and real underwriting awareness.
  • AI Voice Agent: Fully autonomous outbound calling powered by xAI Grok Realtime speech-to-speech API. The AI cold-calls leads, qualifies prospects, handles objections in real time, and books appointments. Human agents can monitor live calls and intercept (take over) at any time.
  • AI Dashboard Assistant: An in-dashboard AI co-pilot with 80+ tool-calling actions accessible by text or voice input. Can search contacts, make calls, send SMS, book appointments, check statistics, manage workflows, generate reports, provide coaching insights, and navigate the dashboard on the user's behalf. Uses Web Speech API for voice input where supported by the browser.
  • Power Dialer: Queue-based single-line power dialing with auto-retry, answering machine detection (AMD), number health rotation, geo-routing, and configurable calling hours enforcement. Includes AI-powered Lead Intelligence scoring and Smart Filters.
  • Pro Dialer (Multi-Line): Up to 4 concurrent outbound call lines with AI-optimized dial ratio pacing, connect rate analytics, wrap-up time configuration, daily max per contact, and same-number cooldown enforcement.
  • Solo Predictive Dialer: Erlang-C M/M/N queue model for automated dial pacing. Includes AI Overflow (when multiple calls answer simultaneously, AI voice agent handles overflow calls). TCPA auto-throttle with rolling 30-day abandon rate tracking, recipient timezone enforcement via area code mapping, recording consent tracking for 12 two-party consent states, agent state machine (Ready/Not Ready/Wrap-Up/Break), and callback queue management.
  • SMS Bot: AI texting only (no dialer). Includes all SMS automation, lead intelligence, Smart Filters, workflow automation, and AI Assistant features.
  • CRM Integrations: OAuth-based connection to GoHighLevel / LeadConnector (primary) and HubSpot (full integration including webhooks, data sync, activity logging, and CRM Card). Additional adapters available for Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho CRM, Insureio, and Zapier.
  • Lead Intelligence: AI-powered contact analysis providing temperature rating (hot/warm/cool/cold), engagement score (0-100), should-respond flag, engagement level, summary, and next-best-actions. Cached with 24-hour TTL; invalidated when new messages arrive. Bulk analysis of ~1,000 contacts in ~30 seconds.
  • Workflow Automation: Event-triggered automation sequences including Speed to Lead, Aged Lead Re-engagement, SMS Response Handler, and Re-engage Cold Leads. Supports AI-generated workflows, manual enrollment via assistant, and cron-based triggers.
  • Phone Number Management: Phone numbers provisioned on isolated sub-accounts. First 5 numbers free with any subscription; local numbers $0.90/mo, toll-free $2.15/mo. Number health monitoring, CNAM caller ID registration, Voice Integrity registration, STIR/SHAKEN attestation, and smart number rotation.
  • A2P 10DLC Compliance: Brand registration, campaign creation, and messaging service provisioning. Sub-users may be required to pay a one-time $19 registration fee.
  • Spam Protection: CNAM (Caller Name) registration, Voice Integrity registration with AT&T/Hiya, T-Mobile, and Verizon, A2P 10DLC registration for SMS deliverability, and smart number rotation that automatically rests flagged numbers.
  • Agency Dashboard: Multi-tenant command center for agency owners (free). Real-time KPIs, per-agent statistics, leaderboards (connect rate, call volume, conversation duration), AI-powered weekly reports with per-agent coaching insights, period comparison, conversion funnel analysis, and call log across all agents.
  • White-Label Branding: Agency owners can customize the dashboard with their own company name, logo, accent color, and font family. Agents under the agency see the agency brand instead of InsuranceGrokBot. Includes branded invite emails and claim-seat pages.
  • Team Management: Account owners can invite team members with role-based access (admin, agent, viewer). Each team member can be provisioned with their own phone numbers for independent calling.
  • Reminders: The AI Assistant can create scheduled reminders stored in Redis with configurable delay. Reminders are best-effort notifications and are not guaranteed to fire at the exact specified time.
  • Training API: RESTful API for exporting call recordings and AI transcripts for training and quality assurance purposes.
