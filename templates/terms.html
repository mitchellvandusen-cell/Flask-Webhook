2. Description of Service

InsuranceGrokBot is a white-label AI-powered sales automation platform built for life insurance agents. The Service connects to your CRM via OAuth and includes the following features depending on your subscription tier:

  • AI SMS Handler: Outbound and inbound SMS automation via Twilio. Handles objections, maintains conversation context, and books calendar appointments. Powered by xAI's Grok LLM with 270+ carrier and deep insurance product knowledge.
  • AI Voice Agent: Fully autonomous outbound calling powered by xAI Grok Realtime speech-to-speech API. The AI cold-calls leads, qualifies prospects, handles objections, and books appointments. Human agents can monitor live and intercept at any time.
  • Smart Dialer (Power Dialer): Queue-based single-line power dialing with auto-retry, voicemail detection, answering machine detection (AMD), number health rotation, and geo-routing. Includes AI-powered Lead Intelligence scoring and Smart Filters.
  • Pro Dialer (Multi-Line): Up to 4 concurrent outbound call lines with AI-optimized dial ratio pacing, connect rate analytics, and configurable dialer settings including wrap-up time and daily max.
  • Predictive Dialer: Enterprise-grade Erlang-C M/M/N queue model for automated dial pacing. Includes TCPA auto-throttle (rolling 30-day abandon rate), recipient timezone enforcement (area code mapping), recording consent tracking for two-party consent states, agent state machine (Ready/Not Ready/Wrap-Up/Break), and callback queue management.
  • GoHighLevel CRM Integration: OAuth-based connection to GoHighLevel / LeadConnector. Bidirectional sync of conversations, contacts, opportunities, and pipeline stages. Additional CRM adapters available for HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, Insureio, and Zapier.
  • Lead Intelligence: AI-powered contact analysis (temperature, score, should-respond flag, next-best-actions). Cached in database; invalidated only when new messages arrive.
  • Phone Number Management: Twilio sub-account provisioning. First 5 numbers free; local numbers $0.90/mo, toll-free $2.15/mo. Number health monitoring, CNAM registration, and spam protection.
  • A2P 10DLC Compliance: Brand registration, campaign creation, and messaging service provisioning through Twilio's A2P infrastructure. Sub-users pay a one-time $19 registration fee.
  • Agency Dashboard: Multi-tenant command center for agency owners. Real-time KPIs, per-agent stats, call log, and sub-user management.
  • Unified Dashboard: Inbox, dialer, AI texting, voice configuration, carrier management, carrier intelligence, Discord team chat integration, and activity logs — all in one platform.
