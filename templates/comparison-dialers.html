Ask Yourself These Questions

What happens after the call?

Your dialer helps you call 100 leads. 85 don't pick up. Now what? With a dialer — nothing. They sit in your CRM until you manually text or call again. With InsuranceGrokBot, the AI texts them automatically, handles their replies, and books the ones who are interested.

What happens at 9pm on a Tuesday?

Your lead replies "yeah I'm interested what times work?" at 9:17pm. With a dialer — they wait until morning. With InsuranceGrokBot, the AI checks your calendar, finds tomorrow's open slots, and books the appointment before you even see the notification.

Who's actually hot?

Your dialer shows you a contact list. You don't know who's ready to buy, who's cold, or who said "stop texting me" three weeks ago. InsuranceGrokBot reads every conversation and tells you exactly which leads need attention right now — and why.

Does it know insurance?

Your dialer doesn't know what term life is. It doesn't know which carriers accept diabetics. It can't explain the difference between IUL and whole life. InsuranceGrokBot has 270+ carriers, live underwriting rules, and deep product knowledge baked in. It sells like a licensed agent.
