Smart Filters That Actually Think

Most dialers sort contacts by "last replied." Ours reads every conversation, scores engagement, and groups leads by AI temperature. A lead who said "not interested" gets classified as cold — not hot because they responded recently.

Zero rule-based fallbacks. If the AI hasn't analyzed a contact yet, it shows "pending" — not a fake score. Classification updates automatically after every new message.

SHOULD RESPOND

Lead is waiting for a reply, asked a question, or expressed interest. You need to act now.

HOT LEADS

Actively buying, asking for quotes, comparing coverage, ready to book an appointment.

WARM LEADS

Engaged, sharing info, genuine interest — but not quite ready to commit yet.

COOL

Went quiet, slow or short replies, soft objections. Still worth a follow-up.

COLD

Said no, stopped responding, or explicitly declined. "No thanks" = cold, not hot.

DO NOT CONTACT

Opted out via DnD flag or stop keywords. Automatically excluded from dialing.
