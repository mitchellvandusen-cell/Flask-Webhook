Getting an EIN is free and takes about 5 minutes. You'll receive it immediately.

How to get your EIN

  • Go to the IRS online application
  • Fill out the form (about 5 minutes)
  • You get your EIN immediately after submitting
  • Save or screenshot the confirmation letter they give you

What they'll ask for

  • Your legal name (or LLC name)
  • Business entity type
  • Mailing address
  • Responsible party's name and SSN
