What is an EIN?

A free 9-digit number from the IRS that identifies your business. Think of it like a Social Security number, but for your business.

How to get one

  • Go to the IRS online application
  • Takes about 5 minutes to fill out
  • You get your EIN immediately after submitting
  • It's completely free

What they'll ask

  • Your legal business name
  • Business entity type (LLC, Corporation, etc.)
  • Mailing address
  • Responsible party's name and SSN

Sole Proprietors: You can use your SSN instead of an EIN, but we strongly recommend getting an EIN for privacy and professionalism.
