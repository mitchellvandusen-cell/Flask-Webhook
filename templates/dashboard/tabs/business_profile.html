EIN Strongly Recommended for Sole Proprietors
An EIN (Employer Identification Number) is a free federal tax ID from the IRS. Twilio's carrier trust network requires a business registration number — an EIN is the safest and most private option. It takes about 2 minutes online and is instant. Your SSN is not accepted.
Do you have an EIN?
You can still register — you'll be asked to confirm before proceeding with your SSN. Or get a free EIN from the IRS in about 5 minutes.
