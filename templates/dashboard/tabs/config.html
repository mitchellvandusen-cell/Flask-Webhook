What is A2P 10DLC?
A2P (Application-to-Person) 10DLC is a US carrier requirement for business SMS on local numbers. Without registration, messages may be filtered or blocked. Registration involves two steps: Brand vetting (business identity, 1-7 days) and Campaign approval (message use-case, 24-48 hours).
Helpful recommendations on getting A2P 10DLC approval
Step-by-step guide: branded email setup, sample messages, campaign descriptions, and tips to maximize approval.
Registration Fee Required

A2P 10DLC registration incurs a one-time fee charged by carriers. The fee depends on your brand registration type. Select your brand type and complete payment to proceed.

Low Volume Standard: EIN required, up to 2,000 msgs/day to T-Mobile, lowest cost.
Sole Proprietor: No EIN required, 1 phone number limit, 1 campaign limit.
Standard: EIN required, highest throughput (6,000+ msgs/day), includes secondary vetting.
