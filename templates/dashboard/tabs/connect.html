Connect Your CRM

Choose your CRM platform and follow the setup guide. Your webhook URL is unique to your account.

{{ request.host_url }}webhook

LeadConnector Setup
1
Click "Connect LeadConnector" in the sidebar to authorize via OAuth.
2
Select your sub-account (location) when prompted.
3
In LeadConnector, go to Settings > Business Profile > Integrations.
4
Create a Custom Webhook workflow: When "Customer Replied" trigger fires → Send Webhook to your URL above.
5
Select a Calendar from the Bot Config tab and set your timezone.
Salesforce Setup
1
In Salesforce Setup, go to App Manager > New Connected App.
2
Enable OAuth, add scopes: api, refresh_token. Copy your Client ID and Client Secret.
3
Generate an Access Token via the OAuth flow or use Security Token + Password method.
4
Your Instance URL looks like: https://yourorg.my.salesforce.com
5
For SMS: Add your Twilio credentials (Account SID, Auth Token, Phone Number) below.
6
Set up an Apex Trigger or Flow that sends a webhook to your URL above when a lead message arrives.
HubSpot Setup
1
In HubSpot, go to Settings > Integrations > Private Apps.
2
Create a Private App with scopes: crm.objects.contacts.read/write, crm.objects.deals.read/write.
3
Copy the Access Token from the Private App page.
4
For SMS: Add your Twilio credentials below. Messages log as Communications in the contact timeline.
5
Create a HubSpot Workflow: When contact property changes → Send Webhook to your URL above.
Pipedrive Setup
1
In Pipedrive, go to Settings > Personal Preferences > API.
2
Copy your API Token and your Company Domain (e.g., "yourcompany" from yourcompany.pipedrive.com).
3
New contacts automatically get a Deal created in your pipeline.
4
For SMS: Add your Twilio credentials below. Messages log as Notes on the Person record.
5
Use Pipedrive Automations or Zapier to send a webhook to your URL above on new activity.
Zoho CRM Setup
1
Go to Zoho API Console (api-console.zoho.com) and create a Self Client.
2
Generate tokens with scopes: ZohoCRM.modules.ALL, ZohoCRM.users.READ. Copy Client ID, Client Secret, and Refresh Token.
3
Select your Data Center (com, eu, in, com.au, jp).
4
For SMS: Add your Twilio credentials below. Zoho also supports native email sending.
5
Create a Zoho CRM Workflow Rule with a Webhook action pointing to your URL above.
Zapier Setup
1
In Zapier, create a new Zap with a Webhooks by Zapier > Catch Hook trigger.
2
Copy the Catch Hook URL that Zapier generates.
3
Optionally create separate Zaps for messages, bookings, and contacts with separate hook URLs.
4
Connect your output actions (SMS by Zapier, Twilio, Google Calendar, etc.).
5
Set up a second Zap that sends inbound messages to your webhook URL above.
Insureio Setup
1
In Insureio, go to Account Settings and find your API Key.
2
Find your Brand/Profile ID from the Brands section (required for lead creation).
3
For bookings: Set up a Calendly webhook or similar and paste the URL as your Booking Webhook.
4
For SMS: Add your Twilio credentials below.
5
Use Zapier or a webhook relay to forward inbound messages to your URL above.
