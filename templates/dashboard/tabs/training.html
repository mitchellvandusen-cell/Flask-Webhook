Tips & Best Practices
Speed to Lead
Responding within the first 5 minutes increases contact rates by 10x. Make sure your webhook workflow is active so the bot responds instantly.
Personalize Your Bot
Set your operator name and bio in the sidebar. The AI uses this to introduce itself naturally and build rapport with your leads.
Warm Call After SMS
Let the SMS bot qualify the lead first, then use the dialer to call. The AI summary gives you context before you even pick up the phone.
A2P Compliance
Register for A2P 10DLC in Voice Config to prevent carrier filtering. Unregistered numbers may have messages blocked or delayed.
