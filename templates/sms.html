Objection Engine

6 Objection Types. Each Handled Differently.

The AI classifies every objection by type and nature (fear-based vs logistical). Fear-based objections get empathy and questions. Logistical ones get practical solutions. No scripted rebuttals — the AI reads the full history and picks a new angle every time.

Not Interested
"No thanks, I'm good"
Something put insurance on their radar. That trigger didn't vanish. The AI finds a completely different angle it hasn't tried yet — never repeats an approach.
Fear-based
Already Covered
"I have something through work"
The AI doesn't challenge it. Most people can't explain what they have. It asks questions that let gaps reveal themselves naturally — employer coverage, portability, actual amount.
Logistical
Need to Think
"Let me sleep on it"
Could be genuine processing or decision avoidance. If they've been "thinking" for months, thinking isn't the problem. The AI figures out which and adapts.
Fear-based
Price / Money
"I can't afford that"
Value problem or cash flow problem? Both are valid. The AI determines which. Never quotes specific dollar amounts over text — that's a hard rule. Redirects to a call for real numbers.
Logistical
Spouse / Partner
"Need to talk to my wife"
Respects who they want to consult. Never minimizes it. Explores whether they can gather info now so that conversation with their spouse is productive.
Fear-based
Busy / Timing
"Not a good time right now"
Respects the timing. The question is whether progress can be made in a way that respects their schedule, or whether to anchor a specific follow-up time.
Fear-based

Persistence is configurable per agent (1-5). After the set number of distinct objection-handling attempts, the AI exits gracefully. No pushy loops.

Lead Type Awareness

Different Leads. Different First Messages.

The AI knows the difference between a lead that just filled out a form 5 minutes ago and one that's been sitting in your CRM for 6 months. The opener changes completely.

Fresh Lead

Just requested a quote. Expecting to hear from someone. Speed-to-lead is everything. The AI texts within seconds while they're still thinking about it.

Re-Engage

Showed interest 30+ days ago, went silent. Life got in the way. Their situation may have changed. The AI reaches out from a fresh angle — not a reminder of what they ignored.

Aged Lead

Purchased aged lead. 30+ days old. They may not remember any form. The AI approaches as a stranger — but one who knows they voluntarily expressed interest.

Very Old Lead

3+ months in the system. Contacted by multiple agents. Numb to pitches. The AI uses a completely unexpected angle — anything that sounds like a standard outreach gets deleted.
