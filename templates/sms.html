Conversation Pipeline

Every Text Moves Through a 6-Stage Pipeline

The AI classifies every message and knows exactly which stage the conversation is in. It builds rapport before qualifying, handles objections logistically before emotionally, and only books when the lead is ready. No guessing. No generic replies.

Outreach

First contact. Adapts opener to lead type — fresh, aged, re-engage, or very old.

Rapport

Builds trust first. Responds to personal details, finds common ground, earns the right to ask.

Qualifying

Discovers goals, obstacles, and impact. Asks why coverage matters to them personally.

Objection

Solves the logistical concern first. If it still sticks, addresses the fear underneath.

Booking

Buying signal detected. Offers specific calendar times. No over-explaining.

Booked

Confirms time, sends invite. Stops selling. Conversation complete.

Objection Engine

6 Objection Types. Logistical First. Fear Second.

The AI classifies every objection by type and nature. It always solves the logistical concern first — can they afford it? Does their spouse actually care? Is their existing coverage enough? Only when the practical answer checks out but they're still resisting does it address the fear underneath. No scripted rebuttals — the AI reads the full history and picks a new angle every time.

Not Interested
"No thanks, I'm good"
Acknowledges exactly what they said first. Then asks one question from a completely new angle — about them, not insurance. After multiple rejections, scales back to genuine human check-in. Never repeats an approach.
Logistical → Fear
Already Covered
"I have something through work"
The AI doesn't challenge it. Most people can't explain what they have. It asks questions that let gaps reveal themselves naturally — employer coverage, portability, actual amount.
Logistical
Need to Think
"Let me sleep on it"
Could be genuine processing or decision avoidance. If they've been "thinking" for months, thinking isn't the problem. The AI figures out which and adapts.
Fear-based
Price / Money
"I can't afford that"
Value problem or cash flow problem? Both are valid. The AI determines which. Never quotes specific dollar amounts over text — that's a hard rule. Redirects to a call for real numbers.
Logistical
Spouse / Partner
"Need to talk to my wife"
Respects who they want to consult. Never minimizes it. Explores whether they can gather info now so that conversation with their spouse is productive.
Fear-based
Busy / Timing
"Not a good time right now"
Respects the timing. The question is whether progress can be made in a way that respects their schedule, or whether to anchor a specific follow-up time.
Fear-based

Lead Type Awareness

Different Leads. Different First Messages.

The AI knows the difference between a lead that just filled out a form 5 minutes ago and one that's been sitting in your CRM for 6 months. The opener changes completely.

Fresh Lead

Just requested a quote. Expecting to hear from someone. Speed-to-lead is everything. The AI texts within seconds while they're still thinking about it.

Re-Engage

Showed interest 30+ days ago, went silent. Life got in the way. Their situation may have changed. The AI reaches out from a fresh angle — not a reminder of what they ignored.

Aged Lead

Purchased aged lead. 30+ days old. They may not remember any form. The AI approaches as a stranger — but one who knows they voluntarily expressed interest.

Very Old Lead

3+ months in the system. Contacted by multiple agents. Numb to pitches. The AI uses a completely unexpected angle — anything that sounds like a standard outreach gets deleted.

Follow-Up Intelligence

Follow-Ups That Actually Work

The AI tracks how many consecutive messages it has sent without a reply. Each follow-up is completely different from the last — different angle, different tone, different approach. It reads its own history and does the opposite of what already failed.

  • Tracks consecutive unanswered messages

  • After 5+ attempts: AI goes unconventional — humor, curiosity, unexpected angles
