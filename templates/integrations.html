BUILT-IN

Carrier Intelligence

The bot doesn't just connect to your CRM. It understands the insurance industry. 270+ carriers recognized by name with product classification, underwriting rules, and gap analysis built in.

270+ Carrier Database

Major carriers (Northwestern Mutual, MetLife, Prudential, New York Life), regional mutuals (Farm Bureau, Grange), specialty carriers (Lemonade, Root), and Canadian carriers (Manulife, Sun Life). Automatically classifies each as guaranteed issue, employer group, bundled policy, or standard.

Live Underwriting Rules

Real-time underwriting data sourced from live Google Sheets covering whole life, term, IUL, and universal life products. The bot knows which carriers accept specific health conditions, so when a lead mentions a medical issue, the bot already knows the landscape before your advisor picks up the phone.

VOICE AI

AI Voice Agent

Real-time speech-to-speech voice conversations powered by Grok. Your leads talk to an AI agent that sounds natural, answers questions, and books appointments mid-call.

Speech-to-Speech

Native G.711 μ-law audio with zero transcoding for carrier-grade quality. Sub-second latency keeps conversations feeling natural. Choose from 7 distinct voice personalities to match your brand.

Inbound & Outbound

Leads call your AI number for instant engagement, or the AI calls leads automatically via CRM webhook triggers. Auto-greeting detects caller intent and routes the conversation accordingly.

Live Calendar Booking

Mid-call calendar checking and appointment booking using the same engine as the text bot. The voice agent queries available slots, confirms the time with the lead, and locks in the appointment in real time.
